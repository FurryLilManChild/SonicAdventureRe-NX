# Super Sonic Instructions

Super Sonic is an extra in SonicAdventureRe-nx: in the port, Sonic can turn Super in his normal
stages, boss fights and adventure fields, not only in the final fight.

## Before you start

- **Unlock:** Super Sonic becomes available once the last story (Super Sonic's story) is cleared
  on your save.
- **Bypass:** to play as Super Sonic without clearing the last story, open the port menu (Minus
  and Plus) and set **SUPER SONIC** to **UNLOCKED**. The setting is remembered between launches.
  - If you unlock him in the middle of an act, he becomes available the next time Sonic spawns
    (the next act, area, or a restart).
- **Character:** Sonic only.

## Controls

Buttons are listed as Dreamcast buttons, with the Switch button they use in the default layout.
If you have remapped the controls in the port menu, use your own layout.

| What | Dreamcast | Switch (default) |
|---|---|---|
| Turn Super | X in a jump | Y in a jump |
| Turn back to Sonic | X in a jump while Super | Y in a jump while Super |
| Start flight | B in a jump while Super | A in a jump while Super |
| Steer in flight | Stick | Left stick |
| Fly up | Hold A | Hold B |
| Fly down | Hold Y | Hold X |
| End flight | B, or land | A, or land |

While Super, Sonic keeps all of his normal moves (homing attack, spin dash, light dash and the
rest) with Super Sonic's look, runs about as fast as with the speed shoes, and plays the Super
Sonic music.

## Where he is available

| Where | Rings needed | Ring drain | Flight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Action stages (story and trial) | 50 | 1 ring a second | Yes |
| Boss fights | 50 | 1 ring a second | No |
| Adventure fields (Station Square, Egg Carrier, Mystic Ruins, the Past) | None | None | Yes |

- **Not available:** Casinopolis' pinball tables, mini games, the Chao gardens, and the Perfect
  Chaos fight (which has its own Super Sonic).
- **Flight height:**
  - In adventure fields he can climb up to 200 units above where he took off.
  - In action stages he can climb to a little above the highest ground in the act.

## When he turns back to Sonic

- You press X (Switch Y) in a jump.
- His rings run out (action stages and bosses).
- He gets knocked down.
- A cutscene starts.
- He boards a snowboard or sandboard, or a ride takes him.
- He leaves the adventure fields (field Super only).

## Good to know

- **Records:** a run in which Sonic turned Super saves no best time, score or ring count for that
  stage. A run without Super saves records as normal.
- **Water:** Super Sonic needs no air, so he cannot drown.
- **Goal:** clearing an act as Super Sonic plays his own goal pose.
